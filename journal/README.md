# Coach journal

One page per day of post-class debriefs with the coach (DeepSeek, through
`server/coach.mjs`): what you said about the class, what the coach
proposed for next time, in order. Committed on purpose — this is the
record the next class is built from, by the app's coach or by whoever
plans the sequence with you next.

The system prompt is the app's own posture data (chakras, muscle work,
cautions, timing) plus your practice journal and mastery; the log keeps
its hash and head rather than repeating it.
